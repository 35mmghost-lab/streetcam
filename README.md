## 🎞️ Key Features

* 🕶️ **Stealth Mode (Daido Method):** Full black-screen shooting for discreet candid photography "from the hip" with haptic vibration feedback.
* 🎞️ **Film Tone Emulations:**
  * **Kodak Tri-X 400** — Classic high-contrast street monochrome.
  * **Ilford HP5 Plus** — Smooth mid-tones and wide dynamic range.
  * **Kodak T-Max 3200** — Deep blacks and high-contrast night aesthetics.
  * **Fujifilm Neopan 100** — Crisp, balanced, fine-grain BW.
  * **Kodak Portra 400 & Cinestill 800T** — Color tone profiles.
* 🌐 **Composition Grids:**
  * Rule of Thirds
  * Golden Ratio
  * Fibonacci Spiral
  * Diagonals & Center Crosshair
* 🎯 **Stadiametric Rangefinder:** Quick visual distance estimation marks (`1.2m`, `2.0m`, `3.0m`).
* ⚙️ **Manual Controls:**
  * Leica Zone Focusing presets (`0.7m`, `1.2m`, `2.0m`, `3.0m`, `5.0m`, `∞`).
  * Exposure Compensation (`-2.0 EV` to `+2.0 EV`).
* 🖥️ **Fullscreen PWA:** Add to Home Screen for a native standalone camera experience without browser chrome.

## 📲 Installation (Add to Home Screen)

### Android (Chrome / Brave):
1. Open [35mmghost-lab.github.io/streetcam](https://35mmghost-lab.github.io/streetcam/).
2. Tap the **three dots menu** (⋮) in the browser.
3. Select **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.

### iOS (Safari):
1. Open the URL in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share button** (⎋).
3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
